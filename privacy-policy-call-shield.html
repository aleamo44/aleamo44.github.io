Privacy Policy - CallShield

Last Updated: January 2025

CallShield ("we," "our," or "us") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Android application ("the App") collects, uses, and protects your information. By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Data Collection

CallShield operates entirely locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. Your privacy is our priority.

Payment Information: Payment information for Premium subscriptions is handled exclusively by Google Play Store. We never have access to your payment details, credit card information, or any other financial data.

Trial Version Management: For trial version management, the App uses your device's Android ID locally to prevent trial abuse (e.g., uninstalling and reinstalling to get multiple trials). This Android ID is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to external servers.

2. Local Data Storage

The following data is stored locally on your device:

  • Whitelist entries: Phone numbers and contact names you manually add to allow calls
  • Blacklist preferences: Country-based blocking settings and your preferences for which countries to allow or block
  • Blocked call statistics: Information about blocked calls including phone numbers and country prefixes for statistical purposes
  • Trial activation data: Android ID used to prevent trial abuse (stored locally only, never transmitted)

All this data remains on your device and is never transmitted to external servers.

3. Contacts Access

CallShield requires read-only access to your contacts to sync them with the whitelist. This allows the App to automatically allow calls from numbers in your device contacts. Contact data is never transmitted outside your device and is used solely for local call screening purposes.

4. Call Information

When calls are blocked, minimal information is stored locally for statistics:

  • Phone number of the blocked call
  • Country prefix associated with the number
  • Timestamp of the blocked call

This data is used exclusively to provide you with statistics about blocked calls and remains stored locally on your device.

5. Permissions Required

The App requests the following Android permissions to function:

  • READ_PHONE_STATE: To detect incoming calls and determine call state
  • READ_CALL_LOG: To access call information for the call screening service
  • READ_CONTACTS: To sync your contacts with the whitelist (read-only access)
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: To notify you when calls are blocked
  • CALL_SCREENING: To set CallShield as the default call screening app, enabling call blocking functionality
  • INTERNET: Required by AdMob for downloading and displaying ads (free version and trial version), by Firebase Crashlytics for sending crash reports, and by Google Play Billing for subscription management

6. How We Use Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Block incoming calls based on your whitelist and blacklist preferences
  • Sync your device contacts with the whitelist
  • Provide statistics about blocked calls
  • Display notifications when calls are blocked
  • Display advertising through Google AdMob (free version and trial version)
  • Verify Premium subscription status through Google Play Billing
  • Identify and fix technical issues through crash reports
  • Prevent trial abuse by tracking Android ID locally (trial version only)

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data.

7. Data Security

All call blocking data, whitelist entries, blacklist preferences, statistics, and trial activation data remain on your device. While we implement reasonable measures, no system is completely secure. You are responsible for safeguarding your device.

Crash reports and diagnostic data transmitted to Firebase are sent over secure, encrypted connections (HTTPS). Subscription verification communications with Google Play are also encrypted and secure.

8. Third-Party Services

The App integrates the following third-party services:

Firebase Crashlytics

Used for crash reporting only. The App uses Firebase Crashlytics to automatically collect crash reports and diagnostic data when the App unexpectedly terminates. This data includes:

  • Device information (model, manufacturer, Android version)
  • Error stack traces (technical information about the error)
  • App state at the time of the crash
  • Crash timestamps
  • Anonymous installation identifiers

This data is anonymous and is used exclusively to identify and fix technical issues, improving the App's stability. The data is handled according to Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google AdMob

Used for advertisements in the free version and trial version of the App. AdMob may collect data such as:

  • Your device's advertising ID
  • IP address
  • Usage information

For more details, please review Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Their use of your information is governed by their own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Ads: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Ad personalization can be controlled in your device settings.

Google Play Billing

Used for subscription management (monthly and yearly Premium plans). The App uses Google Play Billing to manage Premium subscriptions. Subscription status is verified through Google Play's secure API, but no payment information, credit card details, or billing addresses are accessible to our App.

All subscription management, including cancellations and renewals, is handled entirely through your Google Play Store account. All payment processing and data storage is handled by Google Play Store according to Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can manage your subscription at any time through your Google Play Store settings (Google Play Store → Account → Subscriptions).

9. Subscription Data

CallShield uses Google Play Billing to manage Premium subscriptions. The App verifies your subscription status through Google Play's secure API, but we do not store or have access to any payment information, credit card details, or billing addresses.

All subscription management, including cancellations and renewals, is handled entirely through your Google Play Store account. Subscription status information is managed by Google Play Store according to Google's privacy and data retention policies.

10. GDPR Compliance

As all data is stored locally and no data is transmitted externally (except for anonymous crash reports, advertising data as described above, and subscription verification through Google Play), CallShield complies with GDPR requirements.

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have certain rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right to access: All your data is accessible directly within the App
  • Right to rectification: You can modify your whitelist and blacklist at any time within the App
  • Right to erasure: You can delete all app data by uninstalling the application. Subscription cancellation must be done through Google Play Store settings
  • Right to data portability: Your data is stored locally and can be backed up via Android backup services

Legal basis for processing:

  • Local data storage: Consent (by using the App and configuring settings)
  • Crash reports: Legitimate interest (improving app stability)
  • Advertising (free version and trial version): Consent (can be opted out via Google settings)
  • Subscription verification: Contract performance (verifying Premium subscription status)
  • Trial activation data: Legitimate interest (preventing trial abuse)

11. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

12. Your Choices

  • You may modify or delete your whitelist and blacklist directly within the App
  • You may clear blocked call statistics at any time
  • You may disable notifications in the App settings
  • You may opt out of personalized ads via your device's Google settings
  • You may manage or cancel your Premium subscription through Google Play Store settings
  • Crash reporting data is collected automatically and cannot be opted out of while using the App

13. Data Retention

  • Local data: Retained on your device until you delete the App or clear its data through Android settings
  • Subscription status: Managed by Google Play Store according to Google's data retention policies
  • Crash reports: Retained by Firebase for up to 90 days for diagnostic purposes
  • Advertising data: Managed according to Google AdMob's retention policies
  • Trial activation data: Retained locally on your device until you uninstall the App or clear its data

14. Data Deletion

You can delete all app data by uninstalling the application. This will remove:

  • All whitelist entries
  • All blacklist preferences
  • All blocked call statistics
  • All trial activation data (Android ID)

Subscription Cancellation: Subscription cancellation must be done through Google Play Store settings (Google Play Store → Account → Subscriptions). Cancellation will take effect at the end of your current billing period.

Note: Uninstalling the App will not delete crash reports already sent to Firebase, which are retained according to Firebase's retention policies. Subscription data is managed by Google Play Store according to Google's policies.

15. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

16. Contact Us

For questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the dedicated section in the App (Settings → Contact).
