Privacy First

100% Local Processing

All call blocking happens locally on your device. No data is sent to external servers. Your privacy is guaranteed.

Not a Dialer App

CallShield is not a dialer and does not replace your phone's default dialer. It works alongside your existing phone app.

No Contact Access

CallShield does not access or control your contacts. It only processes call notifications locally to determine blocking actions.

Notification-Based

The app works by intercepting call notifications. It doesn't control your phone's dialer or call functions, only filters incoming calls.
